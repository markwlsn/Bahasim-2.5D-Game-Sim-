BahaRescue 2.5D: Metro Deluge

Philippine DRRM First-Responder Adventure

Typhoon waters are surging through the metropolitan delta! You control Captain Bayani, leading the DRRM ground rescue team.

🎮 Controls & Objectives:
  • Move: A / D or Arrow Keys (Mobile: D-Pad).
  • Jump / Swim Up: W / Space (Mobile: ▲).
  • Dive / Duck: S / Down (Mobile: ▼).
  • Interact / Rescue: E or Action Button when near stranded citizens or clogged drains.
  • Floodwater Physics: Wading slows movement; deep floodwater triggers swimming and consumes oxygen!
  • Mission Goal: Rescue all stranded citizens, collect emergency kits, and reach the safe Evacuation Gym!
